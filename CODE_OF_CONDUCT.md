# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

UnClear is a small open-source project. Everyone who participates — through issues, pull requests, or discussions — should feel welcome and respected, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and constructive in feedback
- Assume good intent
- Stay on topic — this project has a narrow, specific purpose

## Unacceptable behavior

Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or any conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting will not be tolerated.

## Enforcement

Violations may be addressed by removing comments, closing issues or PRs, or blocking access to the repository at the maintainer's discretion.

To report a concern privately, send a message to [@michaelsanford](https://github.com/michaelsanford) on GitHub.
